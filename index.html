        How to play

        1. Everyone starts off the board. Take turns to roll the die and move that many squares.
        2. Land at the foot of a ladder and you climb to its top. Land on a snake's head and you slide down to its tail.
        3. Squares zig-zag: 1 to 10 left to right, 11 to 20 right to left, and so on up to 100.
        4. The first player to reach square 100 wins.

        House rules

        • Exact roll to finish: if your roll would take you past 100 you stay where you are.
        • Six rolls again: rolling a 6 gives you another roll.
        • Three 6s forfeit: a third 6 in a row cancels the turn and sends you back to where the turn began.
